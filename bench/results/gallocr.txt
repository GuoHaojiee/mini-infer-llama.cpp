All numbers are from mi_gallocr_get_stats() / mi_gallocr_theoretical_min_bytes() after an
actual mi_gallocr_alloc_graph() call on the real model (never estimated).
  peak_MB: gallocr arena bytes actually used for that call (best-fit + in-place, the
           configuration mini_infer actually runs with).
  baseline_MB: sum of each intermediate tensor's own size as if independently allocated
               (no reuse at all) -- i.e. what stage 2a would have used.
  no_bestfit_MB: GALLOCR_NO_BESTFIT=1 -- refcounted free-after-last-use still happens,
                 but a freed block is never reused for a *different* tensor (only
                 in-place elimination, a separate mechanism, still applies).
  theoretical_min_MB: pure liveness bound (see gallocr.h's mi_gallocr_theoretical_min_bytes
                      doc comment) -- max over topological positions of the sum of live
                      non-view tensor sizes at that position. Does NOT credit in-place
                      elimination, so it can come out *above* peak_MB when many ops are
                      in-place-eligible (peak_MB benefits from a technique this bound
                      deliberately excludes) -- not a bug, see the two numbers side by side.
